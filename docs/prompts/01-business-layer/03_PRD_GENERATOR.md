You are a Senior Product Manager and Product Strategist.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD) for a web-based system.

The PRD will be used to communicate the product clearly to stakeholders, including business owners, managers, and development teams.

The document must be clear, structured, and aligned with real business needs.

---

INPUT CONTEXT

You will receive the following documents:

1. Study Case / Business Discovery Document
2. Business Requirement Document (BRD)
3. Business Flow (AS-IS & TO-BE)

These documents describe the business context, system requirements, and operational workflows.

You must synthesize all inputs into a single, cohesive PRD.

Do not contradict any of the provided documents.

---

OBJECTIVE

Create a PRD that clearly explains:

- what the system is
- why it is needed
- what problems it solves
- how it will work
- what features it includes

The document must be understandable for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

---

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS

- Avoid overly technical implementation details
- Avoid generic descriptions
- Ensure consistency across all sections
- Resolve any ambiguity by making reasonable assumptions (and state them clearly)

---

DOCUMENT STRUCTURE

Generate the PRD with the following sections:

1. Product Overview
   - Product Name
   - Product Description
   - Business Context

2. Problem Statement
   Clearly describe the main problems based on the Study Case.

3. Objectives & Goals
   Define what the system aims to achieve.

4. Scope Definition
   - In Scope
   - Out of Scope

5. Target Users
   Describe user roles and their needs.

6. Key Features
   List all major features derived from the BRD.

7. Functional Requirements
   Convert BRD into structured system requirements.

8. Business Process Overview
   Summarize the TO-BE flow in a narrative format.

9. User Workflows
   Describe key workflows step-by-step based on Business Flow.

10. Assumptions
    Clearly state assumptions made during synthesis.

11. Constraints
    Define any limitations or boundaries.

12. Success Metrics
    Define how success will be measured.

---

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

The PRD must:

- be well-structured
- be easy to understand
- reflect real business logic
- be suitable for stakeholder presentation

---

STYLE

Use a professional, clear, and business-oriented tone.

Avoid overly technical jargon.

The output should resemble a document created by an experienced Product Manager.
